## 3. Our Approach Page (our-approach.html)

### Hero Section
- **Title:** Our Approach
- **Content:** At AI Youth Innovators, we believe in a holistic approach to education that nurtures both intellectual growth and personal development. Our methodology is built on three core pillars:

### Core Pillars Section
- **Pillar 1 (Balanced Learning):** We integrate AI education with essential outdoor and life skills. This ensures children develop a well-rounded skillset, fostering critical thinking, problem-solving, and adaptability in both digital and real-world environments.
- **Pillar 2 (Hands-On Exploration):** Our programs emphasize experiential learning. Through interactive workshops, practical projects, and outdoor adventures, children actively engage with concepts, making learning fun and memorable.
- **Pillar 3 (Future-Ready Skills):** We focus on equipping children with skills relevant for the AI era, including AI literacy, ethical considerations, and creative application of technology, alongside traditional skills like teamwork, resilience, and leadership.

### How We Implement Section
- **Title:** How We Implement Our Approach
- **AI Literacy:** Engaging workshops and projects that demystify AI, teaching foundational concepts, practical applications, and ethical considerations.
- **Outdoor Education:** Adventures that build resilience, teamwork, and a connection with nature, including bushcraft, navigation, and environmental awareness.
- **Life Skills Development:** Practical sessions on cooking, first aid, financial literacy, and communication, preparing children for independent living.
- **Mentorship & Community:** Experienced educators and youth leaders provide guidance, fostering a supportive community where children can learn, grow, and inspire each other.

## 6. Activities Page (activities.html)

### Hero Section
- **Title:** Our Activities
- **Content:** We offer a diverse range of activities designed to develop both practical skills and digital literacy. Our programs balance outdoor adventures, life skills, and technology education to create well-rounded individuals who can thrive in the AI era.

### Category Filter
- **Buttons:** All Activities, Outdoor, Cooking, Digital Skills, AI Tools

### Activities Grid
- **Outdoor Activities:**
    - **Tent Building:** Learn how to set up different types of tents and shelters, understanding the importance of proper site selection and tent orientation for various weather conditions.
    - **Knot Tying:** Master essential knots for various outdoor applications, including securing gear, building shelters, and basic rescue techniques.
    - **Orienteering & Navigation:** Develop map reading and compass skills to navigate through various terrains, fostering independence and problem-solving.
    - **Bushwalking & Nature Exploration:** Discover local flora and fauna, learn about ecosystems, and practice Leave No Trace principles while enjoying the outdoors.
- **Cooking Activities:**
    - **Campfire Cooking:** Learn to prepare simple, delicious, and safe meals over an open fire, emphasizing fire safety and outdoor cooking techniques.
    - **Basic Nutrition & Meal Prep:** Understand the basics of healthy eating and learn to prepare nutritious snacks and simple meals for outdoor adventures.
- **Digital Skills Activities:**
    - **Digital Storytelling:** Create engaging narratives using digital tools, combining text, images, and multimedia to tell compelling stories.
    - **Basic Coding & Robotics:** Introduction to fundamental coding concepts and hands-on experience with simple robotics kits, fostering logical thinking.
- **AI Tools Activities:**
    - **AI Art & Creativity:** Explore AI tools for generating art, music, and creative content, understanding the role of AI in artistic expression.
    - **AI for Problem Solving:** Use AI-powered tools to analyze data, identify patterns, and propose solutions to real-world challenges.
